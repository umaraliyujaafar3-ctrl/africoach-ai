# AFRICOACH AI

AFRICOACH AI is an AI-powered interview preparation platform designed to help African students, graduates, and job seekers improve their interview skills through realistic simulations and personalized feedback.

## Problem

Many young Africans face challenges during interviews due to limited access to coaching, mentorship, and practical interview preparation opportunities.

## Solution

AFRICOACH AI provides:

* AI-powered interview simulations
* Personalized feedback and scoring
* Career-focused practice sessions
* Multilingual support for African users
* Progress tracking and improvement insights
