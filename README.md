# AFRICOACH AI

> **Practice Smarter. Ace Your Next Interview.**

AFRICOACH AI is an AI-powered interview and career preparation platform designed to help African students, graduates, and job seekers build confidence, improve communication, and prepare effectively for real-world interviews.

The platform provides an interactive environment where users can practice interview questions, receive performance insights, track their progress, and access career-focused assistance.

---

## 📌 Overview

Interviews can be challenging, especially for students and graduates who may have limited access to professional mentors or opportunities to practice with realistic interview scenarios.

**AFRICOACH AI** aims to make interview preparation more accessible by providing users with a digital practice environment that they can use whenever they need it.

Instead of simply reading interview tips, users can actively **practice, review their performance, identify weaknesses, and improve over time.**

---

## 🎯 Project Goals

AFRICOACH AI is built around four main goals:

* Help students and graduates become more confident during interviews.
* Provide realistic interview practice without requiring a human interviewer.
* Help users identify areas where their communication and interview performance can improve.
* Provide career-oriented assistance in one accessible platform.

## ✨ Features

### 🎤 Practice Interview

Users can start an interview practice session and answer interview questions one at a time.

The interface is designed to simulate the flow of an actual interview rather than presenting all questions at once.

**Current capabilities include:**

* Interview question presentation
* Next and Previous question navigation
* Interactive interview sessions
* Interview performance tracking

---

### 🤖 AI-Powered Interview Experience

AFRICOACH AI is designed to incorporate AI into the interview preparation process.

The long-term goal is to allow users to interact with an AI interviewer, respond naturally, and receive useful feedback about their answers.

---

### 📊 Performance Dashboard

The dashboard gives users an overview of their interview preparation progress.

It provides information such as:

* Interviews completed
* Average confidence score
* Communication score
* Current practice streak
* Progress over time
* Strong areas
* Areas that need improvement

This allows users to see their development instead of treating every interview session as an isolated activity.

## 🧠 How AFRICOACH AI Works

At a high level, the platform follows this workflow:

```text
User
  │
  ▼
AFRICOACH AI
  │
  ├── Practice Interview
  │       │
  │       ▼
  │   Interview Questions
  │       │
  │       ▼
  │   User Responses
  │       │
  │       ▼
  │   AI Analysis
  │       │
  │       ▼
  │   Feedback & Scores
  │
  └── Career Assistant
          │
          ▼
      Career Support
```

The goal is to turn interview preparation into a continuous learning process rather than a one-time activity.

The React Compiler is not enabled on this template because of its impact on dev & build performances. To add it, see [this documentation](https://react.dev/learn/react-compiler/installation).

## Expanding the Oxlint configuration

If you are developing a production application, we recommend enabling type-aware lint rules by installing `oxlint-tsgolint` and editing `.oxlintrc.json`:

```json
{
  "$schema": "./node_modules/oxlint/configuration_schema.json",
  "plugins": ["react", "typescript", "oxc"],
  "options": {
    "typeAware": true
  },
  "rules": {
    "react/rules-of-hooks": "error",
    "react/only-export-components": ["warn", { "allowConstantExport": true }]
  }
}
```

See the [Oxlint rules documentation](https://oxc.rs/docs/guide/usage/linter/rules) for the full list of rules and categories.
